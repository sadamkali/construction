Frequently Asked Question

You May Ask

Do you handle new building projects in Uganda?
Yes. We provide construction services for residential and commercial projects and can discuss your site, plans, budget and expected timeline.
Can Builders help with home renovations and extensions?
Yes. We can work on renovations, extensions, upgrades and finishing work, depending on the scope of the project.
Can you help me plan my project and prepare a quotation?
Share the location, project type, plans if available and the work you need. We can then discuss the next steps for your quotation.
How do you keep a project moving from start to handover?
We use clear project stages: site discussion, planning, construction and handover, with regular communication throughout the work.
Can I bring my own building plans or architect?
Yes. You can bring plans from your own architect or designer, and we can work from those documents.
What should I prepare before requesting a quotation?
Useful information includes the site location, type of project, approximate size, drawings if available and the work you would like completed.
Do you handle finishing works as well as the main build?
Yes. Our services can include interior finishing, painting and other final works depending on the project requirements.
How can I arrange a site visit?
Send us a message through the Contact page to arrange a discussion about your site and project needs.
Can Builders work on commercial properties?
Yes. We can discuss offices, shops, rentals, developments and other commercial construction or renovation requirements.
How do I contact Builders for a project discussion?
Use the Contact page to send your details and project brief. The Builders team can then follow up with the next step.
