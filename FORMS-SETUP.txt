# Builders website forms
The contact and article enquiry forms are prepared for Formspree.

Create a Formspree account, choose New Form, then copy the endpoint from the Integration page. Replace YOUR_FORM_ID in contact.html and single.html with the ID from your endpoint, for example:
https://formspree.io/f/abcd1234

The forms already use POST submission, named fields, required validation and inline success/error messages.

Formspree setup guide:
https://help.formspree.io/articles/building-your-form/building-an-html-form
